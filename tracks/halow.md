# halow learning track

Status: outline; no hardware-tested tutorial published yet.

## Learning goal

Plan an IP-connectivity exercise and measure delivery, throughput and power on a documented Wi-Fi HaLow setup.

## Before a hands-on guide

- Select hardware, firmware and primary vendor/project documentation.
- Verify regional settings and the authorized test environment.
- Record reproducible inputs and a measurement method.
- Publish sanitized evidence, cleanup and limitations.

Begin with [experiment design](../guides/01-experiment-design.md), then use the [tutorial template](../templates/tutorial.md). Suggest a concrete lab through an issue; this outline makes no hardware recommendation or compatibility promise.
